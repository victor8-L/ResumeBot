Anduril Industries — Data Analyst Intern, Mission Systems (Summer 2026)

About the role
You will work alongside engineers and analysts to turn messy, multi-source data into
decision-grade products for defense customers. Expect to build pipelines against
geospatial and sensor feeds, evaluate model performance, and write up findings that a
non-technical operator can act on.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain data pipelines that fuse open-source, geospatial, and sensor data
- Evaluate machine learning model outputs and characterize failure modes
- Produce written analytic products and brief findings to technical and operational
  stakeholders
- Partner with government and industry subject matter experts

What we're looking for
- Currently pursuing a degree in a quantitative field
- Proficiency in Python; familiarity with SQL and geospatial tooling a plus
- Demonstrated experience working with real, imperfect data end to end
- Interest in national security; ability to obtain a US security clearance
- Strong written communication — you will be read more often than you are heard
